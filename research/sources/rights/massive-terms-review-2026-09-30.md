# Massive upstream rights evidence

- Source: https://massive.com/legal/market-data-terms-of-service
- Official standard Market Data Terms last updated 2025-08-28 (reviewed 2026-09-30).
- Section 1 limits the standard Market Data license to personal, non-business, non-commercial use and says business/commercial purposes are not allowed under that license.
- The terms restrict copying, transmission, publication, and commercial use absent express prior written consent; some datasets can require a separate agreement with the third-party provider. They state data is display-only unless another agreement says otherwise.
- Massive for Businesses terms define separate business service/order forms and third-party agreements; they do not establish that the repository's current account or any specific crypto dataset has that license.
- Scope limitation: exact paid order form, source-specific crypto rights, storage/derivation and AI terms are not in evidence; no use admission is inferred.
